# OnTopRank Credit & 14-Day Testing Pack Engine

Automates participation in OnTopRank's developer exchange network for real iOS & Android app installs, genuine verified reviews, and 14-day closed testing cohorts.

## Core Capabilities
1. **Credit Escrow Lifecycle**:
   - 30 initial credits per app grant.
   - 30 credits held in escrow when a verified developer accepts a testing task.
   - Escrow settled to tester upon AI/screenshot verification; refunded on failure.
2. **Google Play 14-Day Closed Testing Cohort Governance**:
   - Organizes and monitors cohorts of 12-15 real developers testing continuously for 14 days.
   - Evaluates Google Play closed testing production eligibility.
3. **Multi-App Campaign Optimization**:
   - Balances credit earnings and spending between **Hermes Mobile** and **LipoShield**.
   - Applies priority boost multipliers to accelerate ratings.

## CLI Usage

```bash
# Check all app campaigns & credit balances
bin/ontoprank-engine status

# View specific app status & Google Play compliance
bin/ontoprank-engine app com.igor.hermesmobile

# Apply priority boost multiplier
bin/ontoprank-engine boost com.igor.hermesmobile 2.5
```
