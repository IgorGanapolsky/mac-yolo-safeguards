Keep Hermes moving when you step away from your computer.

Hermes Mobile turns your phone into a private remote control for the Hermes AI agent running on your own Mac, Windows, or Linux machine. Follow work live, send messages, and approve or deny risky tool calls before they run.

This is a desktop AI agent controller—not an on-device phone chatbot. Hermes Mobile does not replace or sign in to other AI products.

CONTROL HERMES FROM YOUR PHONE
• Stream live chat with the agent on your computer
• Approve or deny blocked tool calls in one tap
• View and edit standing allow or block rules
• Keep multiple computer profiles without starting over
• See clear connected, reconnecting, stale, and unavailable states

CONNECT YOUR WAY
• Pair securely with a QR code
• Use home Wi-Fi for local access
• Use Tailscale or your configured tunnel for remote access
• Connect to compatible Hermes setups on Mac, Windows, and Linux

PAID-UPFRONT APP
Pay $4.99 once. No ads. No Hermes Mobile subscription.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• Camera access is used only to scan pairing QR codes
• Optional product analytics can be disabled in Settings
• Chat and API credentials remain on the computer running Hermes
• No Hermes Mobile cloud account is required

Requires a computer running a compatible Hermes setup. Remote access requires your own network, Tailscale, tunnel, or relay configuration.
