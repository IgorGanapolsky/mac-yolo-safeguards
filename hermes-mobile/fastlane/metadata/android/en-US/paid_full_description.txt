Keep Hermes moving when you step away from your computer.

Hermes Mobile is the phone remote control for a Hermes AI agent on your own Mac, Windows, or Linux machine—not an on-device phone chatbot. Chat live, watch run status, and approve or deny risky tool calls before they execute. Your agent and API keys stay on the computer you operate.

WHY HERMES MOBILE
• Control a desktop AI agent from your phone (Mac, Windows, and Linux)
• Stream chat and see honest connected / reconnecting / unavailable states
• Approve or block dangerous tool calls in one tap
• Save multiple computers and switch without starting over
• Remote AI agent access via Tailscale, home Wi‑Fi, or your tunnel

CONNECT YOUR WAY
• Pair with a QR code
• Home Wi‑Fi for local access
• Tailscale for cellular and away-from-home
• Works with compatible Hermes setups on Mac, Windows, and Linux

PAID-UPFRONT APP
Pay once. No ads. No Hermes Mobile subscription required for the core app.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• Camera is only for pairing QR codes
• Optional analytics can be disabled in Settings
• Chat and API credentials remain on the computer running Hermes

Looking for on-device “Hermes Agent” (Hen Works and similar phone-local agents)? That is a different product. Hermes Mobile controls a Hermes agent running on your own computer.

Requires a computer running a compatible Hermes setup. Remote access requires your own network, Tailscale, tunnel, or relay configuration.
