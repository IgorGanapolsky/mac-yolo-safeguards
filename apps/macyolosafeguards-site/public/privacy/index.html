Privacy

Last updated November 11, 2026.

The Mac YOLO Safeguards app runs on your Mac. It reads process, memory and disk information locally to show it to you and to clean things up. It does not send that information anywhere, and it has no accounts or tracking.

This website uses Cloudflare Web Analytics to count page views. It does not use cookies and does not track you across sites.

When you buy something, Stripe handles the payment and receives your email address and payment details under their own privacy policies (Stripe). I receive your email address and what you bought so I can deliver it and answer questions. I do not sell or share it.

If you open a GitHub issue, it is public on GitHub. Questions about your data: open an issue at GitHub issues or reply to your purchase receipt.
