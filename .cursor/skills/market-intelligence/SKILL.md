# Skill: Market Intelligence (`/market-intel`)

## Trigger
Use when searching for high-ROI growth partners, competitor moves in the AI-Memory space, or high-intent users on Show HN / GitHub / X.

## Procedure
1. **Intent Mining:** Use `web_search` to find users complaining about "meeting bots" (Otter/Fathom) or searching for "local AI memory."
2. **Partner Scoring:** Rank potential partners by "Reach" $\times$ "Alignment" $\times$ "Reciprocity."
3. **Signal Mapping:** Identify the "Intent Signal" (e.g., Starring a specific MCP server) that triggers a specific tier offer.
4. **Output:** Generate a list of curated targets in `~/Documents/AI-Agent-Sync/Marketing/Partner-Targets.md`.

## Rubric
- **Precision:** No "generic" AI influencers; only builders and practitioners.
- **Alignment:** Targets must have a proven interest in local-first or on-device software.
- **Verification:** Every target must have a verified contact method (DM/Email).
